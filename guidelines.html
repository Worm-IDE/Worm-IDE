Worm IDE Uploading Guidelines

Welcome to the Worm IDE uploading guidelines. Worm IDE is a mod of PenguinMod. We maintain high standards to keep our community safe, creative, and functional. Follow these rules when uploading projects, extensions, or custom blocks.

🚫 Prohibited Content

  • No Malicious Code: Do not upload projects designed to crash browsers, freeze tabs, or exploit system vulnerabilities.
  • No NSFW Material: Keep all assets, text, and project themes appropriate for all ages.
  • No Severe Jumpscares: Flashbangs, sudden loud noises, or extreme horror elements that harm user health are banned.
  • No IP Grabbers: Tracking user locations, IP addresses, or personal network data is strictly forbidden.

🛠️ Technical Standards

  • Performance Optimization: Optimize large loops and heavy rendering to prevent lagging the community page.
  • Asset Compliance: Ensure all imported images, sounds, and text vectors respect copyright laws.
  • Credit Remixes: If your project modifies another user's work, explicitly credit the original creator in the notes.
  • Extension Moderation: Custom JavaScript extensions must undergo review before appearing in the public gallery.

🏷️ Metadata & Presentation

  • Clear Titles: Avoid clickbait, excessive emoji spam, or misleading titles.
  • Accurate Descriptions: Clearly explain what your project or tool does in the instructions sidebar.
  • Proper Tagging: Mark your project with the correct categories (e.g., Tool, Game, Extension, Art).

⚖️ Enforcement

  • First Offense: Temporary warning and unsharing of the violating project.
  • Repeat Offenses: Permanent ban from uploading to the Worm IDE community.
