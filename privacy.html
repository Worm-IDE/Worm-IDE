Privacy Policy

Updated: July 2025

Welcome to my Scratch Mod! Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how we handle information when you use this Mod. We want everyone to understand this, including kids and their families.

What Data We Collect

  • This Scratch Mod lets you load projects from Scratch by URL or .sb3 files.
  • We do not collect personal data like your name, email, or location.
  • When loading projects from Scratch, Scratch’s own privacy policy applies. You can read it here: Scratch Privacy Policy.
  • This Scratch Mod itself does not track you or collect your information.
  • If you use cloud variables in projects, your username and project ID may be temporarily shared with TurboWarp cloud servers that this Scratch Mod uses.
  • This mod does not store this information.

What You Should Know About Privacy

  • Do not share personal information (like your full name, email, or address) inside your projects.
  • Be careful when sharing project files outside this Mod — others can see what’s inside.

Tracking

  • Loading projects from Scratch means you are subject to Scratch’s privacy policies, which may include tracking like Google Analytics.
  • TurboWarp cloud servers used for cloud variables may temporarily log project IDs and usernames, but This Mod does not control or store this data.

About Extensions and Packaged Projects

Custom extensions or packaged projects may connect to external services. This Mod does not control these third-party services and is not responsible for their privacy practices.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy sometimes. Please check here to see the latest version.

Thanks for using this Scratch Mod and staying safe online! 😊
